About Townsend

A second generation of service in Hamilton

Townsend Plumbing & Heating was established in 1947 by Leonard Townsend in Hamilton, and it is still family owned and operated. Many of our plumbers started with us as apprentices and trained in-house.

1947Founded in Hamilton
20Staff members

All of our plumbers are fully licensed, bonded and insured. Six current employees have been with Townsend for 25 years or more.

A fleet of 15 trucks means service is dispatched quickly, day or night.

Honest, reliable service at a reasonable price, and we always leave behind a clean work area.

We work with homeowners, builders and restoration contractors, and many customers come to us through friends and family.
